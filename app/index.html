    Roadmap to the platinum

    Four stages, in order. The roadmap is built from the official trophy descriptions: story chapters and missions first, then the heists and duo work, then the open-world grind, and finally cleanup and 100% completion.

    Stage 1 — Story

    Chapters 1–4 + final mission

    Play the story from Gone Fishing to the final mission. Do the Chapter 1 meditation spot for Positive Mental Attitude before Chapter 1 ends — it is the only missable trophy. Earns: Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby!, Top Of The World, Ma, You Gotta Know When to Get Off, Trust, Family Matters, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked, Snow Storm.

    Stage 2 — Heists & duo work

    With your partner

    Heists and jobs you do together: The Cartel Heist, 5 bank vaults, the armored truck, the jewelry store, one illegal operation per gang, and the duo wanted-level jobs. Earns: Break the Bank, Maximum Withdrawal, A Classic Blitz Play, Sneakthief, Pest Control, Hot Together, Retail Therapy.

    Stage 3 — Open-world grind

    Activities, races & exploration

    Try all 9 activity types, then grind the counts: 250 stunts, 42 shooting-range golds, 14 drone-race wins, 20 race wins, 8 stranger strands, 100 vehicles, 52 areas, 100 WAiNK It spots, 50 challenges, $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent. Earns: Cunning Stunts, Master of None, The Shootist, Top Fun, Earn Cash, Not Kudos, Baited My Tackle, Stick Fiddler, Strangers & Freaks, Fair Game, One Last Thing, Whip It Good, Gunslinger, Catch Me If You Can, The Goodtime State, In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Let’s Go Bowling, Ten Pin, Dime on the Dollar, Treat Yo Self.

    Stage 4 — Cleanup & 100%

    Collectibles & contact strands

    Finish the contact strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), complete 3 card sets, buy all 8 garages, register 10 vehicles, and sweep up the five still-unconfirmed secret trophies. When the last trophy pops, so does the platinum — and A Long Road at 100% completion. Earns: Vice Got a Price!, A Long Road, Road to Rehabilitation, This Was No Boat Accident, The Lone Rider, Supply & Demand, Spin On This!, Don’t Touch My Deck, You WAiNKer!, Little Boxes, On the Spot, plus the 5 UNCONFIRMED methods.
